A History of Surfing

Introduction

The art of surfing called he'enalu in the Hawaiian language was first described in 1769 by Joseph Banks on the HMS Endeacor during the first voyage of Captain James Cook. Surfing was a central part of ancient polynesian culture and predates European contact. The chief was the most skilled with the best board for surfing - similar to the rest of the ruling class. Commoners were not allowed on the same beaches but could gain prestige from their skill.
